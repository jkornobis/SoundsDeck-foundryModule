# 0004 - No 0.5 release before one table session on 0.4

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Status:** accepted, then **waived for 0.5 by the Composer** the same day: he used 0.4 in the world, gave six notes, and
chose *"Release 0.5 now"* - his try-out supplied what a session would have. The rule stands for later versions.

*Originally:* the Composer programmed it (Auditorium on v0.4, note 4, Agile Auditor).

**Context.** Four versions (0.1 to 0.4) were built and merged in one day. Every claim about them is a measurement in a
headless browser - gains read off Web Audio nodes, buttons clicked by a script. **Nobody has heard the deck at a
table.** The next feature should come from what the Composer reached for and did not find, not from another guess.

**Decision.** Work on 0.5 may be built and merged, but **0.5 is not tagged or released until one real session has been
played on 0.4**, and what that session showed is written down first (in this repository's issues, or the knowledge
repository's session log).

**Why a release gate and not a build freeze.** He programmed notes 6 to 12 of the same Auditorium alongside this one.
Read together, the intent is: keep improving, but do not put a new version in front of the table until the current one
has been in front of it.

**Rejected.** Freezing all work until the session. It would idle the notes he programmed, and a merged, unreleased
change costs the table nothing.

**Waived for 0.6.0** (2026-09-24, the Composer's word when offered the release): 0.6.0 carries the #37 fix, and
0.5.2, which the table still runs, can leave music silent after a quick switch. Holding the fix back for a session on
0.5.2 would put a known silence in front of the table, which is the opposite of what this decision protects.

**Waived for 0.6.1** (2026-09-25, the Composer's word when offered the release): 0.6.1 completes the program he set
from the two field studies. His Stream Deck + knobs and the shortcuts can only be checked once it is installed (Foundry
registers shortcuts only at start-up), so holding it back would also hold back the one test that needs his hardware.

**Waived for 0.6.2** (2026-09-25, the Composer's word when offered the release): the first item of his next program -
a sound for chosen players only - and beds renamed "Musiques" in French.

**Waived for 0.6.3** (2026-09-25, the Composer's word when offered the release): the new look (accent colour,
frosted glass, ripple) and the QA pass.

**Waived for 0.6.4** (2026-09-25, the Composer's word when offered the release): late joiners and the next-track
preload (0.7 note 2), the board as tabs, Settings in the menu, and the accent reaching Foundry's own hover colours.
